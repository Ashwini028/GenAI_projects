 An embedding is a list of numbers that represents the meaning of a text. Texts with similar meaning get vectors that are close to each other.

 Cosine similarity tells how close the direction of two vectors is. A value near 1 means very similar, and a value near 0 means not related.

 Chunking means splitting a long document into small pieces. Small chunks give more precise matches in search and RAG.

 Overlap means repeating a little text between two chunks. It keeps the context at the border, so an idea is not lost.

 Metadata is extra information stored with each chunk, such as the file name and the chunk number.
